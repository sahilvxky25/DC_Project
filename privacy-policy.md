# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 21 November 2026**

BitSecure is an interactive learning platform for Data Communications and computer networking. This policy explains what information the website may process, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on how you use BitSecure, the platform may process:

- **Account information:** email address, Firebase user identifier, and authentication-related account details. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase process the information needed to authenticate you.
- **Learning progress:** completed modules, quiz results, achievements, challenge activity, learning streaks, overall progress, and last-visited module where these features are used.
- **Preferences and local data:** theme and other interface preferences, plus locally cached learning data stored by your browser.
- **Payment information:** If you choose to contribute, the hosted Razorpay checkout processes the details needed to complete the transaction. BitSecure's frontend does not ask for or store card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.
- **Cookie preferences:** your optional analytics preference, stored under the browser key `bitsecure.cookieConsent.v1`.

Do not enter passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information into learning activities or free-text fields.

## 2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and restore learning progress, provide platform features, remember preferences, maintain site security, and respond to support requests. Learning progress associated with an account may be synchronized using Firebase so it can be available when you sign in on another device.

## 3. Firebase and other service providers

BitSecure uses Firebase Authentication for account sign-in and Firestore for account-related learning progress. Google sign-in is provided through Google's authentication service. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. When the Support Us payment option is enabled, Razorpay provides the hosted payment checkout and processes the payment method, donor details, transaction information, and related technical data needed to complete the contribution. BitSecure does not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords. Each provider's processing is also governed by its own privacy terms and policies.

## 4. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

BitSecure uses browser storage for essential functionality, including session-related state, preferences, and learning progress/cache. The cookie preference panel offers **Accept All**, **Reject Optional**, and **Customize Preferences**.

The optional analytics preference is recorded so the site can respect your choice if an analytics integration is introduced. **The current consent component does not load an analytics or marketing tracker.** Essential storage is required for core functionality and cannot be disabled through the optional-consent controls. You can reopen your choice using **Cookie Settings** in the site footer. Clearing browser storage may remove locally cached data and preferences.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not describe or operate BitSecure as a data-broker service, and we do not sell personal information. Information is processed by the service providers described above where needed to operate the platform. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to protect the platform and its users.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Account-related learning progress may remain in Firestore while associated with your account. Browser data remains until it is cleared by you or removed by the browser. Exact retention periods depend on the relevant service and are not currently stated as a fixed duration.

The current website does not provide a verified self-service account/data deletion workflow. To request help with access, correction, or deletion, contact the project maintainers using the [BitSecure GitHub repository](https://github.com/sahilvxky25/DC_Project). **Do not include passwords, verification codes, or sensitive personal information in a public issue.** A dedicated privacy contact address should be added here before the project is presented as having a formal privacy-request channel.

## 7. Security

We use Firebase-managed authentication and platform controls to support account security. No website or online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private, sign out on shared devices, and contact the maintainers if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 8. Children's privacy

BitSecure is an educational project and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the project maintainers so the concern can be reviewed.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to allow optional analytics through the consent controls, change browser preferences, and clear local browser storage. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Use the contact route in Section 6 for privacy-related requests.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when BitSecure's features, data practices, or service providers change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes should be reflected here before the relevant feature is released.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project maintainers through the [BitSecure GitHub repository](https://github.com/sahilvxky25/DC_Project). Please avoid sharing personal or account-sensitive information in public discussions. A dedicated privacy email address has not yet been supplied for this project.
