# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the BitSecure community pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, healthy, and collaborative community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect toward other community members.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its learners.
- Respecting different viewpoints, backgrounds, and levels of technical experience.
- Communicating professionally in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Dismissing or targeting contributors because they are beginners or have different levels of experience.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional, collaborative environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and project-related communication channels. It also applies when an individual is officially representing BitSecure or the project in public spaces.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

Maintainers will consider the nature, context, and impact of each incident and aim to respond fairly and proportionately. They are expected to respect the privacy and safety of anyone reporting an incident.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact options on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through this channel or in public discussions. Follow the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security concerns.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Maintainers will respect confidentiality to the extent possible while addressing a report.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply one or more of the following responses, depending on the severity and circumstances:

1. **Correction:** A private or public warning explaining the behaviour that is unacceptable and why it is inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning, with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from interacting in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces for repeated or severe violations.

Maintainers may skip steps when the severity of an incident warrants a stronger response.
